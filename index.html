FitDream – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the FitDream app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Body photos. The front, side and back photos you choose to upload for your analysis and weekly checks.
  • Body measurements and profile details. Height, weight, waist measurement (optional), training frequency, experience level, training limitations you select, and your chosen goal.
  • Training data. Workout logs (weights, reps), monthly strength goals, progress checks and weekly check results you create in the app.
  • Anonymous account identifier. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe, subscription status and purchase identifiers are processed by Apple and our subscription provider RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.
  • Reminders. If you turn on weekly reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device as notifications.

2. How we use your information

  • To analyze your photos and measurements and create your estimated body-fat range, muscle assessment, target physique, workout program and nutrition targets.
  • To compare weekly checks and progress checks and show your progress over time.
  • To verify your subscription and apply free and Pro limits.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Service providers who process data

  • Supabase – stores your photos, measurements, training data and analysis results, and provides the anonymous account.
  • OpenAI – your photos and measurements are sent to OpenAI's API to produce the analysis. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default. OpenAI may retain it for a limited period for abuse monitoring as described in its own policy.
  • RevenueCat and Apple – process subscriptions and purchases.

These providers only process data to provide their service to us. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

4. Storage and retention

Your data is kept for as long as you use the app. Some preferences and your program settings are also stored on your device. A limited usage record (for example, the number of free analyses used and the date of your last weekly check) is kept so that free-tier limits cannot be reset, even after you delete your data. It contains no photos or measurements.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can delete your photos, measurements, training data and results at any time in the app: Home → ⓘ → Delete my data.
  • You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • To ask about or request deletion of any other data, contact us at the address below.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access to stored data is restricted to your own account. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Not medical advice

Body-fat, muscle and nutrition figures are estimates produced by software and are not medical advice or a diagnosis. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new training or nutrition program, especially if you have a health condition or injury.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Please do not upload photos of other people.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
